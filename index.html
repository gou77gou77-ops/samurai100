검객 100연참 개인정보처리방침

시행일: 2026년 10월 8일

검객 100연참(영문 Samurai: 100 Slash, 일본어 剣客 100連斬, 이하 "앱")은 1인 개발자 susuim(이하 "개발자")이 만든 게임입니다. 이 방침은 앱을 이용할 때 어떤 정보가 어떤 목적으로 처리되는지, 얼마나 보관되는지, 이용자가 어떤 권리를 가지는지 설명합니다.

1. 처리하는 정보와 목적

앱에는 회원 가입이 없고, 개발자는 이름, 이메일, 전화번호, 정확한 위치를 수집하지 않습니다. 앱이 처리하는 정보는 다음과 같습니다.

기능항목목적

2. 랭킹이 동작하는 방식

  • 처음 게임을 시작하면 이 기기용 익명 계정이 자동으로 만들어집니다. 이메일이나 Google 계정은 필요하지 않습니다.
  • 한 판이 끝났을 때 그 기록이 내 최고 기록(전체 기간 또는 이번 주)을 넘으면 서버로 자동 제출됩니다.
  • 랭킹에는 닉네임과 태그, 기록, 사용한 검이 모든 이용자에게 공개됩니다. 닉네임을 정하지 않으면 기본 닉네임이 쓰입니다. 실명이나 연락처를 닉네임으로 쓰지 마세요.
  • 서버는 기록이 정상적인 플레이로 가능한지 확인하며, 의심스러운 기록은 공개하지 않고 검토할 수 있습니다.
  • 디버그용 기능을 쓴 기록은 제출되지 않습니다.

3. 기기 안에만 저장되는 정보

진행 상황(골드, 보유하거나 장착한 검, 최고 기록), 랭킹 제출 판단에 쓰는 내 최고 기록, 구매한 상품 ID, 광고 동의 선택은 이용자의 기기 안에 저장됩니다. 이 정보는 개발자에게 전송되지 않으며 앱을 삭제하면 함께 삭제됩니다. 앱을 삭제해도 서버의 랭킹 정보는 남으므로, 앱을 삭제하기 전에 설정의 "내 랭킹 기록 삭제"를 누르시거나 아래 8항에 따라 요청해 주세요.

4. 광고와 동의 (Google AdMob)

앱은 무료로 제공되며 Google AdMob 광고를 표시합니다. 광고 관련 정보는 Google의 개인정보처리방침에 따라 처리됩니다.

  • Google 개인정보처리방침: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • Google의 광고 데이터 이용: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

유럽경제지역(EEA), 영국, 스위스 등 동의가 필요한 지역의 이용자에게는 광고를 요청하기 전에 Google의 동의 화면을 보여 드리며, 선택을 마치기 전에는 광고를 요청하지 않습니다. 맞춤 광고에 동의하지 않으면 비맞춤 광고 또는 제한된 광고가 표시됩니다. 선택은 앱의 설정 화면 "개인정보 옵션"에서 언제든 바꿀 수 있습니다. 모든 이용자는 기기 설정에서 광고 ID를 재설정하거나 삭제할 수 있습니다.

광고 제거 상품을 구매하면 배너와 판 사이 광고가 표시되지 않습니다. 결과 화면의 보상형 광고(골드 2배)는 원할 때만 보는 선택 기능으로 남습니다.

5. 구매

앱 내 구매는 Google Play(이후 Samsung Galaxy Store 출시 시 해당 스토어)의 결제 시스템으로 처리되며, 개발자는 결제 카드 정보를 받지 않습니다. 앱은 구매 확인과 복원을 위해 스토어가 알려 주는 구매 기록을 사용하고, 구매한 상품 ID만 기기에 저장합니다.

6. 보유 기간과 파기

  • 익명 계정, 닉네임, 전체 기간 랭킹 기록: 랭킹 서비스를 운영하는 동안 또는 이용자가 앱에서 삭제하거나 삭제를 요청할 때까지 보관합니다.
  • 주간 랭킹 기록: 해당 주가 끝나고 8주 뒤 삭제합니다.
  • 제출된 개별 기록과 무결성 확인 결과: 제출 후 90일 동안 보관합니다. 검토 중인 기록은 검토가 끝난 날부터 90일 동안 보관합니다.
  • 서버 요청 기록(IP 주소 등): 최대 30일 동안 보관합니다.
  • 랭킹 서비스를 종료하면 서버의 정보를 지체 없이 삭제합니다. 삭제한 전자 정보는 복구할 수 없는 방법으로 영구 삭제합니다.

Samurai: 100 Slash Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 8, 2026

Samurai: 100 Slash (Korean 검객 100연참, Japanese 剣客 100連斬; the "App") is a game made by an independent developer, susuim (the "Developer"). This policy explains what information is handled when you use the App, why, for how long, and what your rights are.

1. Information handled and why

The App has no sign-up, and the Developer does not collect your name, email address, phone number or precise location. The App handles the following.

FeatureInformationPurpose
Leaderboards
(Google Firebase)		An automatically created anonymous user ID; your nickname and four-digit tag; game records (score, kills, play time, sword used, time of the record); app version, game rules version and the store the App came from; app and device integrity results (Google Play Integrity, Firebase App Check); server request logs (IP address, time)Running the leaderboards, showing nicknames, preventing tampered records and abuse

2. How the leaderboards work

  • When you first play, an anonymous account for this device is created automatically. No email or Google account is needed.
  • When a run beats your own best (all time or this week), it is submitted to the server automatically.
  • The leaderboards show your nickname and tag, records and the sword you used to all players. If you do not choose a nickname, a default one is used. Please do not use your real name or contact details as a nickname.
  • The server checks that a record is possible through normal play and may hold a suspicious record for review instead of showing it.
  • Runs played with debugging features are never submitted.

3. Information kept only on your device

Your progress (gold, swords owned and equipped, best records), the bests used to decide what to submit, the IDs of products you bought and your ad consent choice are stored on your device. They are never sent to the Developer and are deleted when you uninstall the App. Uninstalling does not delete leaderboard information on the server; before uninstalling, use "Delete my ranking data" in the settings, or see section 8.

4. Advertising and consent (Google AdMob)

The App is free and shows ads from Google AdMob. Ad-related information is handled under Google's privacy policy.

  • Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

Where consent is required (for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland), Google's consent form is shown before any ad is requested, and no ad is requested until a choice is made. If you do not consent to personalised ads, non-personalised or limited ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time under "Privacy options" in the App's settings. In some US states you can also opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information for targeted advertising there. Any user can reset or delete the advertising ID in the device settings.

Buying Remove Ads turns off banner ads and ads between runs. The rewarded ad on the result screen (double gold) stays as an optional feature you watch only if you want to.

5. Purchases

In-app purchases are processed by Google Play (and, once released there, Samsung Galaxy Store); the Developer never receives your payment card details. The App uses the purchase records the store provides to confirm and restore purchases, and stores only the IDs of products you bought on your device.

6. Retention and deletion

  • Anonymous account, nickname and all-time leaderboard records: kept while the leaderboards run, or until you delete them in the App or ask for deletion.
  • Weekly leaderboard records: deleted eight weeks after the week ends.
  • Individual submitted runs and their integrity results: kept for 90 days after submission; runs held for review are kept for 90 days after the review ends.
  • Server request logs (such as IP addresses): kept for up to 30 days.
  • If the leaderboards are shut down, the server information is deleted without delay and cannot be recovered.

7. Service providers and international transfers

The Developer does not sell or give your personal information to third parties. Your leaderboard nickname and records are, by design, visible to other players. The Developer uses Google LLC (USA) as a service provider for running and storing the leaderboards (Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Cloud Functions, App Check), app integrity checks (Play Integrity), advertising (AdMob) and payments (Google Play). Leaderboard data is stored in Google Cloud's Seoul region (South Korea, asia-northeast3); as part of Google's operations, security, support and ad processing, information may be transferred to and processed in the United States and other countries where Google operates. For transfers out of the EEA, the UK or Switzerland, Google relies on safeguards such as the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses and the EU–US Data Privacy Framework.

8. Your rights and how to request deletion

You can ask to access, correct or delete your information, or to restrict or object to its processing. You can change your nickname in the App's leaderboard screen once a week. Your leaderboard information (anonymous account, nickname and records) can be deleted in two ways.

  • In the App: Settings → "Delete my ranking data" erases it from the server at once. This cannot be undone.
  • By email: if you have already uninstalled the App or cannot delete from it, email gou77gou77@gmail.com with the nickname#tag that was shown on the leaderboard screen. We will delete it within 10 days of receiving the request and let you know.

Information on your device is deleted when you uninstall the App.

EEA, UK and Swiss users: the legal bases are your consent for personalised advertising (which you can withdraw at any time under "Privacy options"), the performance of our service to you for the leaderboards and purchases, and our legitimate interests in keeping the leaderboards fair, preventing fraud and keeping the service secure. You also have the right to data portability and to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

US users: the Developer does not sell personal information for money. Ads served through AdMob may involve sharing for targeted advertising; where your state law provides the right, you can opt out through "Privacy options" or the device's ad settings.

9. Children

The App is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country) and does not knowingly collect their personal information. If we learn that a child's information has been processed, we will delete it without delay.

10. Security

  • All traffic between the App and the server is encrypted (HTTPS), and stored data is encrypted by Google Cloud.
  • The App cannot write leaderboard data directly; records are submitted only through server-side validation.
  • Access to the management consoles is limited to the Developer.

Changes to this policy will be posted on this page with a new effective date. Significant changes affecting your rights will be announced on this page and in the App update notes at least 7 days before they take effect.

広告削除を購入すると、バナー広告とプレイ間の広告が表示されなくなります。結果画面のリワード広告(ゴールド2倍)は、見たいときだけ見られる任意の機能として残ります。
